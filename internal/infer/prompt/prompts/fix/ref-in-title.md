A `$Name` inside an anchor's title, the part in parentheses (or after the colon in the section
form). Titles are not hashed, so a dependency stated there is invisible to change detection.
- Move the dependency into the body: take the reference out of the title (keep a short plain-word
  title), and, when the body does not reference that name already, cite it in the body's sentence
  that relies on it.
- Do not change what the anchor requires.
